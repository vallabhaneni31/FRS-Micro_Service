# Motivity — HR Page Guide

---

## 1. Dashboard
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Dashboard

- Your home screen when you log in
- Shows today's live attendance summary — how many employees are Present, Late, and Absent
- Has a progress bar that fills up as more people check in through the day
- Shows a 30-day attendance trend chart so you can spot patterns over the past month
- Shows attendance broken down by department so you know which teams are in and which aren't

---

## 2. Analytics
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Analytics

- Shows detailed attendance data for any individual employee or department
- Select an employee from the dropdown and choose a time period — Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, This Month, YTD, or a custom date range — then click **Apply**
- Shows 4 key numbers at the top:
  - **Attendance Rate** — what % of days the employee was present in the selected period
  - **Present Days** — how many working days they actually came in
  - **Late Arrivals** — how many times they arrived late
  - **Avg Working Hours** — average hours per working day
- Shows an **Hourly Entry / Exit Activity** chart — a bar chart showing what time of day people are entering and leaving
- Shows a **Monthly Calendar** on the right — each day is colour coded: green for Present, yellow for Late, pink for Absent

---

## 3. Attendance
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Attendance

- The main day-to-day attendance screen used every day by HR
- Shows every employee as a row with their check-in time, check-out time, total hours, break time, and status (Present / Late / Absent)
- Pick any date using the date picker at the top to view past records
- Search by employee name or filter by department to narrow down the list
- Click the arrow on any employee row to expand it and see:
  - Exact break start and end times
  - Proof photos captured by the camera at entry and exit
- Use the **Export** button (top right) to download the data as an Excel file or CSV for payroll or reporting

---

## 4. People / Visitors
**How to get there:** Sidebar → People/Visitors

- Shows a list of everyone the cameras have seen — both employees and unrecognised visitors
- Useful for checking who entered the office on a particular day
- Lets you see when a visitor arrived, what they looked like (photo from camera), and how long they were on the premises
- Unrecognised faces are shown separately so you can identify and register them if needed

---

## 5. Employees
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Employees

- The master list of all employees in your organisation
- Add a new employee by clicking **+ Add Employee** at the top right and filling in their name, employee code, email, department, and shift
- Click any employee's name to open their full profile which shows:
  - Their department, shift, and contact details
  - Their complete attendance history day by day
  - Whether they have completed face enrollment or not
- Use the filter chips to quickly find employees who:
  - Haven't been enrolled yet
  - Haven't given consent for biometric data
  - Are newly joined this month
- Select multiple employees at once to bulk-assign a department or shift, or send enrollment invites to all of them together

---

## 6. Enrollment
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Enrollment

- Manages the process of registering each employee's face so the cameras can recognise them
- Shows a list of all employees with the current status of their enrollment invite:
  - **Pending** — invite sent, employee hasn't opened it yet
  - **Opened** — employee clicked the link in the email
  - **In Progress** — employee has started taking photos
  - **Completed** — employee finished all photos and submitted
  - **Approved** — face is active on the camera, system can now recognise them
  - **Expired** — link expired before they completed it, needs to be resent
- Resend invites to individuals or bulk-resend to everyone who hasn't completed enrollment
- Once approved, the employee's face is live on the Jetson camera device and attendance is captured automatically

---

## 7. Departments & Shifts
**How to get there:** Sidebar → Departments & Shifts

- Where you set up the structure of your organisation before adding employees
- Has three tabs:

**Departments tab**
  - Create departments like Engineering, Sales, HR, etc.
  - Assign employees to their respective department
  - See how many employees are in each department and their attendance today

**Shifts tab**
  - Create shift schedules like Morning Shift, Night Shift, etc.
  - Set the start time, end time, and grace period (number of minutes after start time before someone is marked Late)
  - Choose which days of the week are working days for that shift
  - Toggle Flexible ON if there is no fixed start time — employees on flexible shifts are never marked Late

**Roster tab**
  - See all employees grouped by department and shift in one combined view
  - Shows each employee's check-in time and attendance status for today

## Quick Cheat Sheet

| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| See today's overall attendance | Dashboard |
| Check one employee's attendance history in detail | Analytics |
| See all staff check-in and check-out times for a day | Attendance |
| Download attendance data for payroll | Attendance → Export button |
| Add a new employee | Employees → + Add Employee |
| See who hasn't enrolled their face yet | Employees → Unenrolled filter |
| Send a face enrollment invite | Enrollment |
| Resend an expired enrollment invite | Enrollment → Resend |
| Create a department or shift | Departments & Shifts |
| Check who visited the office | People/Visitors |
